Questions groups ask

Will LiTC compete with our group?

No. We don't run a competing membership. Our job is to send people toward groups like yours and help you do more together.

Do we have to change our name or brand?

No. Your group stays your group. Joint events carry both names.

Who can join?

Any group that serves Latino professionals, students, founders, or communities in Massachusetts.

Do we need to be a formal organization?

No. ERGs, student groups, and informal communities are all welcome.
