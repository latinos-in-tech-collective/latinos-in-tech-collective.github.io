A network of Latino networks.

Latinos in Tech Collective connects the Latino professional groups, ERGs, and communities that already exist, so we can build collective wealth and ownership together. Starting in Boston.

Talent is everywhere. Ownership isn't yet.

Latino residents drove about a quarter of Massachusetts economic growth over the last decade. But that growth hasn't turned into the same share of companies, capital, and wealth.

Plenty of groups already do important work on careers, mentorship, and community. We don't want to rebuild any of it. We want to connect it, so the people in all these groups can find each other, work together, and eventually invest together.

How we grow

We move in phases. Each one earns the next.

  1. 1Happening now

    Connect

    Link Latino groups across Boston and give their members one shared network.

  2. 2Next

    Grow

    Reach the critical mass of people, groups, and partners where real collaboration starts.

  3. 3Later

    Own together

    Pool knowledge and capital to invest in Latino ventures, and build wealth that stays in our communities.

Our goals

From our community charter.

Create opportunities

Open doors through connections, projects, and visibility members couldn't reach alone.

Provide support

Reliable encouragement, resources, and community for every member, at every stage.

Promote resources & mentorship

Connect members to the guidance and relationships they need to grow.

Drive capital & seed funding

Champion access to funding so Latino founders can turn ideas into ventures.

Build forward, always

Move with intention — every action pushes the community ahead, not sideways.

Foster open-source collaboration

Share knowledge freely across skills — ideas should flow, never stay siloed.

Hold each other accountable

We commit to following through on our word, and to each other.

Stay accessible

Open and welcoming, regardless of background, experience level, or resources.

As members, we will

  • Treat others how we want to be treated
  • Contribute back, not just take
  • Own our actions, mistakes, and commitments
  • Advocate for and lift up one another
  • Collaborate rather than go it alone
  • Stay open to new ideas and feedback
  • Share knowledge and support others
  • Practice empathy before reacting
  • Respect people's time, ideas, and boundaries
  • Make sure everyone feels welcome and valued
  • Show up consistently for the community

We will not embody

  • Disruptive or reckless conduct
  • Putting others down or taking advantage
  • Dismissing others' ideas or backgrounds
  • Flooding the community or expecting more than we give
  • Harassment of any kind
  • Undermining fellow members or their work
  • Leaving others out of opportunities or spaces
  • Stirring division or drama
  • Racism, classism, sexism, or discrimination
  • Exclusive circles that alienate other members
