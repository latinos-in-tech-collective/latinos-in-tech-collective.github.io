Our mentorship program pairs mentees with someone a few steps further down the road they're walking — structured enough to be useful, loose enough to actually fit real life.

3 cohorts a year
$0 cost, always

How it works

01

Apply and pick a focus area

Interview prep, leveling up into senior or staff, breaking into a new discipline, or something else entirely — you tell us.

02

We match you

Pairings are made by hand, not an algorithm — based on focus area, industry, and what you said you actually need.

03

Twelve weeks of regular check-ins

Every pair sets their own cadence, usually biweekly. We send a light structure so no one's guessing what to talk about.

04

Close it out together

A short wrap-up call at the end — what worked, what to keep doing, and whether to keep going informally.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to already work in tech to apply as a mentee?

No. Students and career-changers make up a large share of every cohort. What matters is having a specific-ish focus area, even a rough one.

What if my mentor match isn't working out?

Tell us. We'd rather rematch in week two than have anyone quietly disengage for ten more weeks.

Is this only for people in the U.S.?

No — mentors and mentees join from across the Americas and beyond. Time zones are part of the matching process.

Can I do it again after my cohort ends?

Yes. Plenty of people mentor for a cohort or two, then become mentors themselves the next.
