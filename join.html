Before you ask

Is there a cost to join?

No. Membership, mentorship, and events are free. Some in-person meetups may ask you to cover your own food or a small venue fee.

What if my city doesn't have a chapter?

You're still welcome in the Slack and at virtual events — and you can always be the one who starts a local chapter.

Do I need to be Latino to join?

This community is built by and centered on Latino professionals, but allies who show up respectfully are welcome too.

I'm not currently working in tech. Can I still join?

Yes — students, career-changers, and anyone trying to break in are exactly who the mentorship program is built for.
