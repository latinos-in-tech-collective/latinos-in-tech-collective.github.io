What we do

Boston has dozens of Latino groups doing great work, often without knowing about each other. We're the connective tissue between them.

Map the groups

Find out who's already out there, from ERGs and professional associations to student and founder communities.

Bring them together

Host joint events that no single group could pull off alone, like our mixer at Boston Tech Week.

Share across groups

Pass along events, opportunities, and resources so a member of one group can reach all of them.

Learn to own

Run shared sessions on investing, equity, and ownership, open to every group in the network.
